03

Try It: Splitting a Signal by Frequency

The core idea behind hybrid images and blending in Project 2. A Gaussian blur keeps the low frequencies; subtracting it from the original leaves the high frequencies. Drag the slider to change σ.

Original signal Low-pass: G ∗ f High-pass: f − G ∗ f

Larger σ blurs more, so the low-pass curve gets smoother and more of the detail moves into the high-pass curve.
